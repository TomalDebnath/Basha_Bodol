TENANT OFFER FEATURE
====================

1. Back up your database.
2. Open phpMyAdmin -> basha_bodol -> SQL.
3. Run tenant_offer_migration.sql once.
4. Restart the Node.js server.

New moving bookings now require a tenant offer amount in BDT.
Providers see the offer in their Mover dashboard and can Accept offer or Reject.
Only an accepted offer can move through In Progress and Completed.
Legacy bookings are kept compatible by the migration.

The project backend in this ZIP is Node.js/Express (not PHP).
